# PluckDL

A simple downloader for TikTok, Threads, and X.

Paste a link, choose what you want to download, and you're good to go.

## Features

- Download TikTok videos without watermarks, plus photos and carousels.
- Download videos and images from Threads and X.
- Automatically detect the platform from the link.
- Download image carousels as a ZIP file.
- Light and dark themes.
- Indonesian and English language support.
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop.
- No ads, no account required.

## Usage

1. Copy a post link from TikTok, Threads, or X.
2. Paste it into PluckDL and click **Check Link**.
3. Choose your preferred format and download.

Only public posts are supported.
